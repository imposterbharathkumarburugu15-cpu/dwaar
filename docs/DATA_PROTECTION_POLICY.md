# Data Protection & Privacy Policy

## 1. Compliance
In compliance with the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act 2023 and AICTE Data Governance guidelines:
- **Purpose Limitation**: Personal and institutional data are collected exclusively for regulatory scrutiny.
- **Data Minimization**: Only strictly necessary fields are stored; raw sensitive scans can be purged post-cryptographic verification.
- **Storage Security**: AES-256-GCM envelope encryption for private keys and evidence payloads.

## 2. Retention and Deletion
- Audit log records are retained permanently for statutory audit compliance.
- Rejected draft applications are purged after 90 days of inactivity.
- Public scans are processed purely in-memory and never persisted to disk.
