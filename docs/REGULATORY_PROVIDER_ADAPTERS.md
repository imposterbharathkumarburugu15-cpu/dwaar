# Regulatory Authority Provider Adapters

## Supported Regulatory Authorities
- **AICTE Central Exchange**: Verification of Extension of Approval (EoA), intake capacities, and faculty cadre ratios.
- **NBA Accreditation Registry**: Verification of Washington Accord Tier-I / Tier-II accreditation status and validity windows.
- **UGC Master Portal**: University Section 2(f) and 12(B) recognition status.
