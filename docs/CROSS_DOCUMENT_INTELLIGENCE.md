# Cross-Document Consistency Intelligence

## Overview
Cross-document intelligence compares declarations and extracted evidence across all documents submitted within an application package.

## Supported Inconsistency Checks
1. **Name Matching**: Compares Society / Trust names in incorporation certificates against the application.
2. **Survey Number Alignment**: Verifies exact land parcel coordinates across Land Sale Deed, Non-Agricultural (NA) order, and Fire Safety NOC.
3. **Chronological Validity**: Flags impossible timelines, such as land purchase dates predating society registration.
4. **Engineering Tolerance Checking**: Compares architect-certified built-up area with approved municipal plans.
