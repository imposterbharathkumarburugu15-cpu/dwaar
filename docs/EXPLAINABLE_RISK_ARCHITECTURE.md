# Explainable Multi-Dimensional Risk Architecture

## 6 Risk Dimensions
1. **Document Authenticity (Weight: 25%)**: Cryptographic integrity, pHash image reuse detection, C2PA manifest provenance.
2. **Institutional Identity (Weight: 15%)**: Entity registration, trust deed continuity, promoter identity consistency.
3. **Regulatory Compliance (Weight: 25%)**: Land acreage, built-up area, faculty cadre ratio, required laboratory infrastructure.
4. **Data Consistency (Weight: 15%)**: Cross-document agreement across affidavits, NOCs, and municipal records.
5. **Historical Evidence (Weight: 10%)**: Past inspection findings, show-cause notices, prior rectification performance.
6. **External Verification (Weight: 10%)**: Real-time government exchange validation (API Setu, DigiLocker).

## Human In The Loop Principle
PRAMANA risk scores strictly assist human regulatory officers. The engine never automatically accepts or rejects applications.
